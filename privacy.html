GhMX — Privacy Policy

This policy explains the different privacy practices of the GhMX iOS and Android apps. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

01 · Overview

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the GhMX applications for iOS and Android (the “Apps”), developed by SlantedCorp (“we”, “our” or “us”), handle information when you use them.

Platform difference: the iOS app uses Firebase Analytics for limited app-usage and external-link measurements. The Android app does not use analytics and does not provide notifications.

Neither App requires a GhMX account. We do not ask for your name, postal address, phone number or payment information.

02 · Information

2. Information We Process

2.1 iOS app usage and link analytics

The iOS app only uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the app is used. This includes automatically generated app-usage events and an event when you tap an external website link.

For an external-link event, we send the link’s internal identifier, its displayed name and the destination website’s domain. We do not deliberately include your name, email address or the full page you visit.

Firebase may also process technical information such as a Firebase installation identifier, app instance information, device and operating-system details, app version, language, time zone, approximate region derived from an IP address, and event timestamps. Google processes this information on our behalf to provide analytics reports.

2.2 Android app

The Android app does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics or another analytics platform. It does not send app-usage or link-click analytics to SlantedCorp.

2.3 Information we do not request

  • No GhMX user account, password or profile
  • No precise GPS location
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No payment or financial information
  • No advertising profile or cross-app tracking by SlantedCorp
03 · Purpose

3. Why We Use Analytics

For the iOS app only, we use aggregated analytics to:

  • Count use of external links and understand which listings are useful
  • Understand general feature usage and app performance
  • Improve event information, navigation and future versions of the App
  • Detect technical problems and protect the reliability of the service

We do not sell analytics information and do not use it to make decisions about employment, credit, insurance or other eligibility matters.

04 · On-device data

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The Apps may store some information locally so their features work correctly. In the iOS app this includes:

  • Your selected light, dark or system appearance preference
  • Your saved or favourite program sessions
  • Cached schedules, exhibitor details and other event content for offline access
  • Local notification requests for reminders you choose to create

This local information is not uploaded to SlantedCorp. It can generally be removed by deleting the relevant App. iOS calendar entries and delivered notifications may need to be removed separately from Apple Calendar or Notification Center.

05 · Permissions

5. Calendar and Notifications

5.1 Apple Calendar in the iOS app

If you choose “Add to Apple Calendar” in the iOS app, it asks for calendar permission and creates the selected event in your default calendar. The app uses this permission only to provide the feature you requested. SlantedCorp does not receive your calendar contents.

5.2 Local reminders in the iOS app

If you choose an event reminder in the iOS app, it asks for notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your device. The iOS app does not currently use remote push notifications.

5.3 Android app

The Android app does not provide local or remote notifications and does not request notification permission.

You can change calendar and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

06 · Services

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Firebase and Google Analytics

The iOS app uses Firebase and Google Analytics, which are provided by Google. The Android app does not use these analytics services. Google’s processing for the iOS app is governed by applicable Google and Firebase terms and policies. Learn more at Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

6.2 GitHub

Schedules, exhibitor information, food-vendor images, the venue map and this privacy page may be delivered from GitHub or GitHub Pages. When content is requested, GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information such as an IP address, user agent, requested file and request time. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6.3 External websites

The App contains links to GhMX, exhibitor and other third-party websites. When you open one, that website or its app may collect information under its own privacy policy. SlantedCorp does not control those third parties.

07 · Disclosure

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We disclose limited information to service providers described above so they can provide hosting and analytics. We may also disclose information when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App or our legal rights. We do not sell your personal information.

08 · Retention

8. Retention and International Processing

Analytics information is retained according to the retention settings and policies of Google Analytics and Firebase. Hosting providers may keep technical logs for their own security and operational purposes. These providers may process information in Australia and other countries where they operate, which may have different privacy laws.

09 · Choices

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can decline calendar or notification permission without losing access to the main program, map and exhibitor features. You can contact us to ask a privacy question or exercise rights available under applicable law. Because our analytics reports may not directly identify you, we may not always be able to associate aggregated analytics with an individual request.

10 · Children

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly request personal details from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

11 · Security

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12 · Changes

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App, our providers or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy and update the date shown above.
