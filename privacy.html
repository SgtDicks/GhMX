GHMX — Privacy Policy

GHMX is built with a simple rule: we don’t collect your personal data, and we don’t sell it.

Last Updated: 18 November 2025
Applicable Frameworks: Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) & global privacy best practices

01 · Overview

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the GHMX application (“the App”), developed by SlantedCorp (“we”, “our”, “us”), handles information about its users in line with the Australian Privacy Act and recognised global privacy standards.

GHMX does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information about you. Your data remains on your device and under your control.
02 · No collection

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The GHMX app is intentionally designed to avoid collecting personal data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details
  • User-generated content, messages, or uploads
  • Analytics or usage tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs
  • Device identifiers, hardware details, or IP addresses
  • GPS or other location information
  • Advertising IDs, cookies, or cross-app tracking data

We do not build user profiles, track behaviour, or engage in any data monetisation activities.

03 · On-device data

3. Local Device Storage

To improve performance and loading times, the App may store limited, non-personal information locally on your device, such as:

  • Cached content (e.g., map tiles, images)
  • Basic interface preferences or settings

This locally stored data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to SlantedCorp
  • Can usually be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage/cache
04 · Third parties

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Hosting (GitHub)

App content and static assets (for example, images, schedules, or configuration files) may be hosted via GitHub Pages.

GitHub’s servers may receive standard technical information required to deliver content (such as generic server logs), but GHMX does not access, collect, or store this information, and we do not use it to identify you.

4.2 Mapping (Leaflet)

The App uses Leaflet, an open-source mapping library, to display map content. Depending on configuration, map tiles may be served by third-party tile providers.

Leaflet itself does not collect personal data about you. Any tile provider only receives what is technically necessary to respond to map tile requests (such as standard web request information). GHMX does not receive or store this data.

4.3 Embedded Social Media Links

The App may contain external links to social platforms, such as:

  • Facebook
  • Instagram

When you tap one of these links, it will open in your browser or the relevant social media app. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. GHMX does not send personal data to these platforms.

4.4 Services We Do Not Use

GHMX does not use:

  • Push notification services
  • Payment processors
  • Advertising networks or ad trackers
  • Analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics)
  • Social media SDKs or sign-in integrations
05 · Children

5. Children’s Privacy

GHMX does not collect personal information from any users, including children. The App is suitable for general audiences and does not include features that require users to provide personal details.

06 · Security

6. Security

Because we do not maintain a database of user personal information, there is no central store of your data for an attacker to target. We nonetheless take reasonable steps to ensure that the App and its hosted content are kept secure and are not tampered with.

07 · International

7. International Considerations

This Privacy Policy is designed in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and follows broadly recognised global privacy principles.

Since GHMX does not collect or store personal information, there is no international transfer of your personal data.

08 · Changes

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable laws. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of the App after any update to this Privacy Policy will be taken as acceptance of the revised terms.
